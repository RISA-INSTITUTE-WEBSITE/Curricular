# Applied Physics curriculum change record

## 27 November 2026 — Version 1.0 design proposal

- Renamed the Physics course family to Applied Physics with twelve career-specific titles; kept existing directory and module codes.
- Expanded from the original PHY101 outline to the three-level, 21-module catalogue (20 compulsory modules and one optional module).
- Preserved original PHY101 content order; updated its title to Classical Mechanics & Properties of Matter.
- Incorporated Level 2 mathematical alignment, PCS102 integration, career electives and deferrals. No separate PCS201 or standalone Complex Analysis module.
- Incorporated deferred Electronics topics, Circuit Theory, approved electronics elective packages, thermodynamics/statistical mechanics including microstates, macrostates and combinatorics, and Quantum Mechanics 2.
- Classified PHY304 as an optional module, independent of compulsory prerequisites. Completed draft details for remaining Level 3 modules, including nuclear stability curves, gamma decay and particle physics.
- Kept syllabus files as independent reusable module definitions, not duplicated under every career title.
- Review required for new detailed outlines, workload, credit equivalence, learning outcomes, assessment rubrics and institutional approval. No website changes.
