## Applied learning and scope

Select two optical instruments (C1 magnifier, C2 microscope, C3 telescope, C4 camera) and one three-topic package (G1 lasers, H1 fibre, I1 sensors). Use supervised low-risk optical labs, ray tracing, diffraction and polarisation simulations. Introduce optical wave equation phenomenologically before PHY301.
