## Applied learning and scope

Include factorials, permutations, combinations, binomial/multinomial coefficients, indistinguishable particles and Stirling approximation within combinatorics. Teach Legendre transforms, phase space and classical Hamiltonians within core, not dependent on PHY304. Calorimetry, gas laws, heat conduction, cooling and distributions; choose one four-topic package.

## Elective packages

Select one package (or approved equivalent). All topics below are elective catalogue topics unless specifically identified as bridging in the investigation description.
