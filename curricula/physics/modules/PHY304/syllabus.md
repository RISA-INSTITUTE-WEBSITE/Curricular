## Applied learning and scope

Entire module optional; students who select it complete its internal core and one two-topic H1–H4 package. No required module may depend on this module; introduce needed Hamiltonian and Legendre concepts locally. Detailed outline newly proposed for review.

## Elective packages

Select one package (or approved equivalent). All topics below are elective catalogue topics unless specifically identified as bridging in the investigation description.

### H1 — Theoretical Mechanics

- Generating Functions
- Separation and Action–Angle Variables

Investigation: Canonical transformation study (include Hamilton–Jacobi equation as bridging).

### H2 — Celestial Mechanics

- Central Force and Orbital Dynamics
- Perturbed Orbital Systems

Investigation: Orbital simulation.

### H3 — Engineering Dynamics

- Constrained Mechanical Systems
- Coupled Mechanical Models

Investigation: Mechanical dynamics model.

### H4 — Computational Dynamics

- Symplectic Numerical Integration
- Phase Portraits and Introductory Chaos

Investigation: Compare numerical Hamiltonian methods.
