## Applied learning and scope

Core retains stability curves, gamma decay, fundamental particle physics and radiation safety. Select one three-topic N1–N7 package; use public data and simulations, no unauthorised source handling or reactor experiments. Sections B,C,J preserve approved topics, with advanced particle topics classified elective under the approved framework; other eight sections newly drafted.

## Elective packages

Select one package (or approved equivalent). All topics below are elective catalogue topics unless specifically identified as bridging in the investigation description.

### N1 — Nuclear Energy

- Reactor Energy Conversion and Safety Principles
- Nuclear Power Plant Energy Systems
- Reactor Safety and Waste Stewardship

Investigation: Public energy and safety case data.

- Nuclear Medicine and Diagnostic Imaging

## Assessment and evidence

Use the common assessment framework, with measurable analytical, practical and interpretation outcomes mapped to each section before delivery. Assess chosen electives at comparable depth and notional hours. Require reproducible work, sources, verification and appropriate uncertainty or error analysis. Formal credits, detailed outcomes, rubrics and teaching hours remain subject to academic review.
