## Applied learning and scope

Include impulse functions and elementary transfer functions in core. Verify transforms, BVP solutions and numerical convergence. Choose one three-topic package. No standalone complex analysis.

## Elective packages

Select one package (or approved equivalent). All topics below are elective catalogue topics unless specifically identified as bridging in the investigation description.

### M1 — Dynamical Systems

- Advanced Nonlinear Differential Equations
- Nonlinear Stability and Bifurcation Analysis
- Introduction to Chaotic Dynamical Systems

Investigation: Driven pendulum model.

### M2 — Signal Analysis

- Transfer Functions, Poles and Zeros
- Frequency Response and System Stability
- Discrete Fourier Transform and Fast Fourier Transform

Investigation: Scientific signal filtering.

### M3 — Mathematical Physics

- Frobenius Method
- Special Functions in Mathematical Physics
- Introduction to Green's Functions

Investigation: Analytical and numerical boundary value problem.

### M4 — Computational Methods

- Advanced Runge–Kutta and Adaptive Methods
- Finite Difference Methods for Partial Differential Equations
- Numerical Stability, Convergence and Error Analysis

Investigation: Verified numerical solver.

### M5 — Diffusion & Transport

- Advanced Heat and Mass Diffusion Equations
- Advection–Diffusion Equations
- Reaction–Diffusion Systems

Investigation: Temperature or concentration model.
