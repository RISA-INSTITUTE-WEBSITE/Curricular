## Applied learning and scope

Teach Hermite, Legendre, Laguerre functions and tensor products as needed, irrespective of MAT301 elective. Labs on oscillator, spin, orbitals, spectroscopy and approximation errors. Choose one three-topic package.

## Elective packages

Select one package (or approved equivalent). All topics below are elective catalogue topics unless specifically identified as bridging in the investigation description.

### Q1 — Theoretical Quantum Physics

- WKB Approximation
- Introduction to Quantum Scattering Theory
- Advanced Operator and Perturbation Methods

Investigation: Compare exact and approximate potentials.

### Q2 — Atomic & Optical Physics

- Zeeman Effect
- Stark Effect
- Quantum Transition Rates and Selection Rules

Investigation: Atomic spectra in external fields.

### Q3 — Nuclear Quantum Physics

- Quantum Tunnelling in Nuclear Decay
- Nuclear Spin and Parity
- Quantum States in Nuclear Potential Models

Investigation: Published nuclear energy or decay data.

### Q4 — Quantum Materials

- Quantum Mechanics of Electrons in Periodic Potentials
- Quantum Confinement
- Electron Spin and Magnetic Interactions

Investigation: Nanoscale electron states.

### Q5 — Quantum Computing

- Qubits and Quantum Gates
- Quantum Entanglement
- Quantum Measurement in Information Systems

Investigation: Small quantum circuit simulation.

### Q6 — Computational Quantum Mechanics

- Numerical Schrödinger Equation Solutions
- Numerical Diagonalisation of Hamiltonian Operators
- Time Evolution of Quantum Wave Packets

Investigation: Numerical quantum system.
