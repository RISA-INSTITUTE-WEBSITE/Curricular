## Applied learning and scope

Validate circuit theorems, transients, semiconductor characteristics and sensor signals using current-limited low-voltage labs; LTspice or equivalent. Introduce basic biasing and feedback wherever needed for core applications. Choose one seven-topic package in pathways.md.
