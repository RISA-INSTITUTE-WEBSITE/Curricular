Verify programme hours using a real calendar, contact time, guided labs, assessment and independent study; validate all packages against equivalent outcomes and complexity. ## Resources and academic integrity

Python, NumPy, SciPy, SymPy, Matplotlib, Jupyter and Git support computational learning throughout. Electronics uses appropriate circuit simulation and supervised low-voltage equipment; optics requires appropriate laser safety; thermal labs avoid uncontrolled heat and pressure; nuclear labs use public datasets or appropriately authorised facilities. All project work requires version history, raw data or documented provenance, working, validation, citations, uncertainty analysis and oral defence when appropriate. Simulations cannot substitute for every required experimental competency.
