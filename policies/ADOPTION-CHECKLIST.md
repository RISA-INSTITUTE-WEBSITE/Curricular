# Policy adoption and readiness checklist

## Confirmed design decisions

- [x] MCC, LCC, CCC certificate types.
- [x] Applied Physics MCC: all prescribed sections, activities, assessments and practicals complete; assessments >=55%.
- [x] Applied Physics CCC: all compulsory modules and prescribed electives; overall >=60%; PHY307 >=70%.
- [x] Applied Physics twelve career pathways; 20 compulsory modules; PHY304 optional.
- [x] School/course/independent module/topic architecture and career-oriented elective framework.
- [x] Practical-first learning and evidence-based assessment principles documented.
- [x] Central policy directory and traceable policy identifiers prepared.

## Decisions requiring explicit ratification

- [ ] LCC level average >=60%, individual module pass >=55%, Level 3 project >=70%.
- [ ] MCC 55% applied to each required graded summative assessment, final assessment and overall module grade; treatment of formative/pass-fail items.
- [ ] Pathway-based module completion interpretation (selected electives vs full catalogue).
- [ ] Interim equal-module average, future credit-weighted average and optional-module credit equivalence.
- [ ] Project-specific PCS102 and PHY307 assessment rubrics; exact grade aggregation and reassessment rules.
- [ ] Progression rules, grading classifications if adopted, appeal timelines, moderation plan and retention schedule.
- [ ] Approving authority, functional role assignments, effective date and version transition.
- [ ] Course-specific schedules beyond Applied Physics; preserve existing proposed DevOps requirements until reviewed.

## Technical and delivery gates

- [ ] Academic review and institutional adoption recorded.
- [ ] Curriculum hours, credit equivalence, elective workload and measurable outcomes validated.
- [ ] Laboratory risk assessments, supervision and accessible delivery verified.
- [ ] Website/LMS server-side eligibility, evidence and moderation implemented.
- [ ] Certificate signing, QR verification, privacy, revocation and audit trail implemented.
- [ ] Acceptance tests executed and independently reviewed; no live issuance before readiness.

A checked design decision does not establish accreditation or successful software implementation.
