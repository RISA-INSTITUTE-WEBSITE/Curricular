## 1. Content classes

Core (C): required foundational content. Elective (E): selected specialised content; once prescribed it is required for that pathway. Revision (R): diagnostic verification and remediation, not automatic exemption. Practical (P): required applied competency, potentially career-specific. Bridging (B): prerequisite support for pathway change or missing competencies.

Topic-level electives sit within a compulsory module. Module-level electives are complete optional modules unless a formally approved pathway specifies otherwise. Optional modules cannot be hard prerequisites for compulsory modules; required supporting concepts must be taught within compulsory content.

## 2. Progressive specialisation

Level 1 common foundation and career exploration through practicals; Level 2 common intermediate theory plus introductory electives; Level 3 advanced core plus career-oriented packages and independent research. Applied Physics has twelve named pathways in its [pathway register](../curricula/physics/pathways.md). All learners must meet common outcomes regardless of specialisation.

## 3. Selection and changes

Record selected package identifiers and version, approved substitutions and bridging outcomes. Offer coherent packages rather than unrelated topic collections. Compare workload, mathematical complexity, practical requirements and assessment difficulty; equal topic counts alone are insufficient. Permit changes where prerequisites, capacity and workload allow; provide necessary bridging and update the award pathway before certification. Do not waive completed or missing requirements silently.

## 4. Applied Physics requirements

Use the [module register](../curricula/physics/README.md) and [certification schedule](schedules/applied-physics-certification.md). Six required Level 1, seven Level 2 and seven Level 3 modules; PHY304 optional for all current pathways. The independent research project PHY307 is compulsory. The optional-module credit equivalence decision remains pending. No standalone Complex Analysis or PCS201 module is required by this curriculum.

## 5. Progression and award distinction

Progression requires demonstrated prerequisite competence under the approved programme plan. A certificate threshold is not automatically a progression rule; do not impose new progression or retake requirements without adoption. LCC is not a required prerequisite certificate for CCC unless explicitly adopted; CCC has its own overall requirements. Record electives and research specialisation on transcripts. Do not create unsupported professional or accreditation claims.
