## 1. Assessment design

Assess learning outcomes, not clicks or time watched. Publish assessment instructions, criteria, weighting, permitted resources and required evidence. Use knowledge checkpoints, analytical tasks, practical laboratories and final module assessments as applicable. Provide clear feedback and equitable accommodations without lowering essential competencies.

## 2. Evidence and identity

- Authenticate submissions to the learner and assignment; preserve timestamps, rubric version, attempt ID and assessor decision.
- Require working, raw measurements or documented data provenance, reproducible code/configuration, test results and uncertainty/error analysis as applicable.
- Independently verify practical outputs with controlled tests, reproducibility checks or supervised observation. A self-reported CI badge, screenshot or video-completion flag is insufficient.
- Use oral defence or individual practical verification where appropriate, particularly for research and suspected authorship concerns.
- Disclose permitted collaboration, AI/tool assistance and citation requirements before assessment. Automated similarity or AI detection alone is not proof of misconduct.
- Keep private learner work and evidence outside public GitHub repositories. Use scoped credentials and isolated execution.

## 3. Marking and moderation

Use outcome-mapped rubrics, comparable difficulty across elective packages and calibrated assessors. Document moderation of borderline grades, final assessments, projects and disputed results under an approved sampling or independent review plan. Avoid unauthorised grade changes. Maintain audit history and version-controlled marking rules. Grades become certification inputs only when authorised and final.

## 4. Misconduct, remediation and appeals

Potential misconduct triggers a confidential review, not automatic guilt. Notify the learner of the concern, provide opportunity to respond, record evidence and a reasoned decision, and offer independent appeal. Withhold issuance for unresolved material cases; do not erase historical attempts. Apply approved sanctions and reassessment rules proportionately. Restore eligibility after authorised remediation or successful appeal if all conditions are met.

## 5. Academic quality gates

Before delivery: confirm assessor competence, rubrics, equivalent elective workload, accessible delivery, assessment security and practical feasibility. Before award: verify completion, grades, integrity clearance and moderation. Record any unresolved issue and block issuance where mandatory. Detailed moderation frequency, retention periods, appeals time limits and reassessment procedures require institutional adoption.
