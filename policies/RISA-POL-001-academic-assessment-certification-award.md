## 1. Purpose

Recognise verified academic competence rather than attendance, video completion or payment. The three award types are Module Completion Certificate (MCC), Level Completion Certificate (LCC), and Course Completion Certificate (CCC). The curriculum owner confirmed all three types and the Applied Physics MCC and CCC thresholds.

## 2. Mandatory principles

- Completion is based on the learner's assigned curriculum version and approved pathway requirements, not the entire elective catalogue.
- Complete every prescribed section or its applicable core/selected elective content, lesson, activity, assignment, assessment and practical. A wholly unselected elective section is not a missing requirement. Any required introductory or revision checkpoint remains mandatory.
- Passing and completion are separate; no aggregate mark, payment or manual status override substitutes for a missing required item, failed applicable threshold, unresolved integrity case or required safety gate.
- Apply published rubrics and assessment plans, record verified results and moderation decisions, and require identity and integrity verification before awarding.
- Certificates are institutional completion credentials; they are not automatically accredited degrees or professional qualifications.

## 3. Award types and Applied Physics thresholds

| Award | Confirmed requirements | Additional implementation decisions |
|---|---|---|
| MCC | All prescribed module sections, activities, assessments and practicals complete; assessments passed at 55% or higher | Proposed interpretation: every required graded summative assessment, final assessment and weighted module result >=55%; formative and pass/fail tasks follow published competency criteria |
| LCC | Award type confirmed: completion of an academic level | Proposed: all required modules and electives complete; each module passes; level average >=60%; PHY307 >=70% for Level 3 |
| CCC | Complete all compulsory modules and their accompanying prescribed electives for a named career pathway; net average >=60%; PHY307 research project >=70% | Module-level pass gates and verified practical/assessment records inherited; use the approved averaging method |

Thresholds are inclusive. Do not round a failing result upward to confer eligibility. The LCC numerical rules, the summative-assessment interpretation and precise averaging method require explicit ratification before automated issuance. No new thresholds for other courses are set by this policy.

## 4. Assessment and results

Use the course-specific assessment schedule and publish component weights, marking criteria, required tasks, pass gates and reassessment rules before teaching. Physics and Mathematics design weights are documented in the [Applied Physics schedule](schedules/applied-physics-certification.md). Do not force those weights on project-only modules or other disciplines. No unapproved weights may be substituted.

Record original attempts, moderated grades, invalidations and approved reassessments; use the final authorised grade under the applicable reassessment policy. No retake cap, fee, grade cap or appeal deadline is established here; adopt and communicate such rules before enforcement. An unresolved or missing official grade is not zero or a pass for certification purposes.

## 5. Average and optional module treatment

Proposed interim arithmetic mean of all required final module marks at the relevant level/course, including PHY307 for Level 3 and CCC. Required modules counted once; no averaging of level averages with unequal module counts. Optional PHY304 is listed separately and does not rescue a failing compulsory average. After formal credit allocation, adopt a credit-weighted average with versioned transition rules. Compare unrounded values; display rounding is separate. Until the method is approved, show an indicative average but do not issue an award that depends on it automatically.

## 6. Award authorisation

The assessment function confirms academic evidence; an independent moderator or designated authorised reviewer validates the award recommendation; the awards function records the authorised decision and issues a verifiable credential. Separate grading, moderation and issuance permissions where feasible. The platform shall fail closed for incomplete or unresolved data. Revoke or correct awards only through the documented records procedure; allow fair review and appeals.
