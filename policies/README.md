## Authority and applicability

Institution-wide principles apply once adopted. Numerical award thresholds in the Applied Physics schedule do not automatically replace another course's approved assessment scheme. For example, the [Cloud & DevOps proposed assessment design](../curricula/Cloud%20%26%20DevOps/assessment-and-delivery-framework.md) retains its separate proposed 70% and safety/recovery gates. Any conflict requires academic governance review rather than silent override.

Use the approved policy version, applicable course schedule and learner's assigned curriculum version together. Record approvals, effective dates, transition rules and exception decisions in controlled history. A lower-level schedule may specify stricter requirements but cannot waive an adopted common safety or integrity gate. Unresolved requirements must block automatic issuance.

Do not represent RISA completion certificates as accredited degrees, professional licences or formally recognised qualifications without documented authority. Do not put student identity records, assessment evidence, signing keys or private credentials in this repository.
